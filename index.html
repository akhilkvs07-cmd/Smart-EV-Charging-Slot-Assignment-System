How VoltFlow makes its decision

Four simple steps, from arrival to results.

01
Collect vehicle details

Each vehicle has a battery level, arrival time, charging time and urgency.

02
Choose who goes next

Smart mode favours urgent and low-battery vehicles. FCFS follows arrival order.

03
Find an available charger

The simulator chooses the charger that can start the vehicle earliest.

04
Compare the outcome

Check average waiting time, longest wait and how busy chargers were.

Remember: this is a learning simulator. It uses sample or manually entered values, not live vehicle or charger data.
